# Institutional fixed-income research workflow

A simplified corporate-credit workflow starts with issuer scope and horizon, then examines business and financial risk, debt structure and liquidity, market pricing, peer value, macro sensitivity, scenarios, and thesis invalidation. A bond can be fundamentally weak yet attractive if compensation is sufficient; a strong issuer can be unattractive at too tight a spread. Research must therefore connect issuer risk to price.

The prototype uses leverage, coverage, free cash flow, liquidity, spread, CDS, historical spread position, rates, and refinancing context. It ends in a conditional research conclusion, not an autonomous trade. A real process would additionally require covenant analysis, legal entity mapping, security documentation, curve construction, optionality, liquidity, primary-market context, portfolio mandate constraints, and current licensed data.
