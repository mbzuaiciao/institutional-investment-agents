# Human oversight and controls

Human-in-the-loop means a consequential transition requires an explicit decision. Human-on-the-loop means a person supervises a running system and can intervene. Merely showing the final output is neither. Effective review needs a clear decision, accessible evidence, surfaced exceptions, and authority to request revision or reject.

The prototype represents approve, revise, and reject decisions with rationale and conditions. Its deterministic reviewer is only a reproducibility device. Institutional deployment would need named accountability, entitlements, escalation paths, model-risk governance, record retention, and controls against reviewers rubber-stamping fluent output.
