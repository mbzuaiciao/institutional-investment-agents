# Literature map

This map favors primary sources and records only references with well-established bibliographic identity.

## LLM agents and tool use

- Yao et al. (2023), *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models*, ICLR. Interleaves reasoning traces with actions and observations.
- Schick et al. (2023), *Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools*, NeurIPS. Studies self-supervised API-use examples.
- Wang et al. (2024), *A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents*, Frontiers of Computer Science. Broad taxonomy; useful orientation rather than an evaluation standard.

## Retrieval-grounded generation and verification

- Lewis et al. (2020), *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks*, NeurIPS. Canonical retrieval-plus-generation architecture.
- Gao et al. (2023), *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models: A Survey*, arXiv:2312.10997. Taxonomy and open problems.
- Min et al. (2023), *FActScore: Fine-grained Atomic Evaluation of Factual Precision in Long Form Text Generation*, EMNLP. Decomposes generations into atomic claims and checks support.

## Multi-agent systems and evaluation

- Wu et al. (2023), *AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation*, arXiv:2308.08155. A framework reference, not evidence that multi-agent designs are superior.
- Liu et al. (2023), *AgentBench: Evaluating LLMs as Agents*, ICLR 2024. Multi-environment agent benchmark.
- Zhou et al. (2023), *Language Agent Tree Search Unifies Reasoning, Acting, and Planning in Language Models*, ICML 2024. Search-based agent reasoning.

## Judge limitations and oversight

- Zheng et al. (2023), *Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena*, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks. Documents judge biases while developing scalable comparison methods.
- Bai et al. (2022), *Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback*, arXiv:2212.08073. Structured critique and revision with explicit principles.
- Amershi et al. (2019), *Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction*, CHI. Human-centered interaction guidance relevant to review gates.

## Finance

- Araci (2019), *FinBERT: Financial Sentiment Analysis with Pre-trained Language Models*, arXiv:1908.10063. Domain adaptation for financial language.
- Yang, Liu, and Wang (2023), *FinGPT: Open-Source Financial Large Language Models*, arXiv:2306.06031. Open financial-LLM research program.

Finance-agent literature evolves rapidly and often uses incomparable private datasets. This repository therefore avoids claiming a settled institutional architecture and focuses on falsifiable, local harness comparisons.
