# Verification and critique

Critique and verification solve different problems. The critic asks whether the thesis is fragile, incomplete, or explained by an alternative hypothesis. It records a target, severity, conflicting evidence, and resolution status. The verifier checks mechanical properties: whether support objects exist, citations overlap the claims they purportedly support, calculations are traced, and contradictions remain visible.

A critic can create false challenges; a verifier can enforce the wrong rule. Neither is an oracle. Their value must be measured through risk recall, contradiction detection, unsupported claims, revisions, and incremental workflow cost.
