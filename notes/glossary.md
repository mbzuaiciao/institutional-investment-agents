# Glossary

**Agent** — A policy that observes state, chooses actions, receives environmental results, transitions state, and terminates.

**Harness** — The surrounding system that constrains inputs, tools, state, routing, verification, approvals, and traces.

**Evidence** — A source object with stable identity and provenance that may support or contradict a claim.

**Claim** — An atomic assertion labeled factual, calculated, inferred, or judgmental, with support pointers and confidence.

**OAS / spread** — Compensation above a benchmark after relevant adjustments. This prototype uses a simplified yield spread, not full OAS.

**Spread duration** — Approximate percentage price sensitivity to a 100-percentage-point spread change; used here with basis-point scaling.

**DV01** — Approximate currency price change for a one-basis-point rate move.

**Relative value** — Assessment of compensation for risk versus peers, history, instruments, or scenarios.

**Critic** — A role that challenges assumptions, missing risks, and alternative explanations.

**Verifier** — A mechanism checking specified support and consistency properties; not a truth oracle.

**Audit trace** — Ordered events sufficient to reconstruct important actions, objects, actors, and dependencies.

**Human gate** — A structured approval, revision, or rejection decision required before a workflow transition.
