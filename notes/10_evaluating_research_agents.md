# Evaluating research agents

Final prose is an incomplete endpoint metric. Controlled evaluation should fix the question, data, tools, and evidence, vary one harness feature, and measure correctness, evidence quality, agent behavior, and operational burden. Repeated seeds reduce dependence on one convenient case.

This benchmark combines directional synthetic ground truth, risk-factor recall, task completion, claim support, citation validity, unsupported claims, tool calls, and audit steps. Its composite quality score is transparent but normative; readers should inspect component metrics. Model-call cost is not fabricated because the default backend makes no paid calls. Steps and tool calls are cost proxies.

Threats to validity include synthetic realism, author-designed ground truth, correlated metrics, deterministic policies, and small samples. The benchmark is suited to testing software behavior and research-process hypotheses, not claiming live alpha.
